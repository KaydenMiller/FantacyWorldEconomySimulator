# Autonomous Build — Decisions Log

This log records educated guesses and design-gap resolutions made while building the World Economy Simulation autonomously (per the user's directive to build all phases to a functioning simulation, fixing/guessing as needed and noting choices). Each entry: what was ambiguous, the choice, and why.

## Operating mode
- Build each phase → test (warning-clean build + green tests) → commit → merge to `master` → next phase.
- A **CLI** (`WorldEcon.Cli`) is built for exercising the simulation headlessly (no UI), per the user's instruction.
- Reviews are lighter in autonomous mode (self-verify + tests; deeper review only at risky integration points) to maintain velocity.

- **2026-11-22 — River drawing: smoothed courses and flow direction (uncommitted).** Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-river-drawing-design.md`, plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-22-river-drawing.md`. Author picked approach B (straighten then smooth the region-cell route; up to half a region cell of drift, about 15 km on earth-5km) over A (round corners only) and C (store a simplified line from the 5 km tile course at generation; changes the saved format and summary hash), with C as the next step if B is not enough. `RiverChains` joins the drawn region edges into chains (source → mouth, confluence or land; the larger inflow continues at a confluence), simplifies with Douglas–Peucker at 0.5 region-cell angle and cuts corners with Chaikin (2 iterations), keeping endpoints and confluence points exactly.   - **Deviations from spec §6 to §9 as implemented (from `task-5-report.md` §10, `task-4-report.md` fix round, `task-7a`, `task-7b`, `task-6b`):**
    1. **Ocean (§6 step 5):** ocean = every connected below-sea-level component with area ≥ `Tiles.MinOceanAreaKm2` (100,000 km², about 4,600 tiles at the default grid), not only the largest component; separate seas count, small enclosed basins stay land. Reason: a single-component rule leaves second oceans as land and lets one deep enclosed basin decide the sea level.
    2. **Sea level (§6 step 5):** the integer level whose area-weighted land fraction is closest to `SeaLevel.LandFractionBp`, ties to the lower level, replaces the quantile of tile elevations. Reason: with the ocean rule, a quantile miscounts enclosed basins (59.0 % land at a 50 % setting on the 92,162-tile test world). Result at the default preset: 30.00 % of area (29.09 % of tiles), sea level 201 m (globe 197 m); 50 % small world: 50.00 % of area. Areas are integers in units of mean tile area / 2²⁰ with `Int128` products (no overflow at radius 1 to 1,000,000 km).
    3. **Lakes (§6 step 7):** lakes fill to the evaporation balance level, not all-or-nothing at the spill level, with a merge tree of nested basins (a full inner lake spills into its sibling or parent). Reason: the all-or-nothing rule gave 17.6 % lakes of land and no terminal lakes below the rim. A basin shallower than the minimum depth at its saddle is absorbed.
    4. **Flow (§6 step 7):** flow leaving a full lake is inflow − min(inflow, evaporation), booked at the lake's exit tile; the spec says flow = precipitation × area summed upstream. Evaporation per tile = (`EvaporationBaseMm` + `EvaporationMmPerDegree` × max(0, tile °C)) × tile area (the globe stage used a basin-mean temperature).
    5. **Drainage (§6 step 7):** a tile drains to its flood parent (lowest filled neighbour, equal levels resolved by flood pop order; flats drain in id order), not to the lowest neighbour with ties to the lowest id (review minor T4-M2, open).
    6. **Outlet breaching (new):** a basin whose top lake is full drains when its depth ≤ `Tiles.BreachDepthM` × (outflow / `Tiles.MajorRiverFlow`)^`BreachExponentPermille`/1000 (exponent 0.5). Breach channels are carved: the path from the lowest tile to the outlet is set to T = max(lowest elevation, tile sea level); nested pits are filled; a basin with a floor below sea level is breached partially (it stays a full lake at level T). After all basins are resolved, each channel is carved downstream of the spill tile until a tile at or below T, a water tile, or a tile bordering a lake or dry bed above T; a final pass raises each non-water land tile to at least its non-water downstream tile. This modifies stored elevations (author decision owed, below). Without carving, the review measured 169,306 tiles more than 10 m below a tile on their drainage path (largest gap 726 m); with carving, 0 tiles more than 1 m below on 5 seeds. `BreachDepthM` was retuned 300 → 600 m after carving (300 gave lake 2.50 % on seed 42 and 3.02 % on seed 7, above the 2.5 % bound; 450 gave 2.20 / 2.66 %).
    7. **Minimum basin depth at tile level:** `Tiles.MinBasinDepthM` 10 m replaces `Hydrology.MinBasinDepthM` (2 m). Depth alone: 2 → 5/10/20/40 m gave 7.39/6.53/5.68/4.08 % lakes of land.
    8. **Landform rules (§6 step 8):** Valley uses the maximum over the relief neighbourhood (not the immediate neighbours); "low" for Wetland = at or below the surrounding mean; the surrounding mean is r passes of neighbour averaging; the biome slope is clamped so relief-based main types come only from landform. `Tiles.MountainHeightM` was removed; `Biomes.MountainHeightM` is the single height threshold.
    9. **Summaries (§6 step 9, §8):** summary river edges drop short systems with the globe rule (`Hydrology.RiverMinSystemEdges` 3 unless in the top `RiverTopTierPercentileBp` of flows; coastal stubs 26 to 29 → 0 on seed 42); the region summary's largest lake counts kind Lake only.

Determinism comes from the seeded RNG + stable iteration order (explicit `OrderBy`); branch/compare/what-if already work via the file-copy snapshot/branch/compare built in Plan 1b (which need no log). **Trade-off:** we give up O(1) log-fork branching and replay-under-different-ruleset; those can be layered later by adding log-replay on top. The `DmAction` log (Plan 4) is still built as the party-effect channel + audit record, but is not (yet) the source of truth. Rationale: dramatically simpler, gets to a functioning sim, determinism still holds for forward runs.
